Catalog

Refresh re-reads the trainings repository and registers new or changed trainings. For each lab it creates an empty pool (size 1 by default, no sandboxes are allocated), creates a training instance that starts now and never expires, and adds an unpublished mission to the course track. It does not allocate sandboxes, does not publish missions and does not release training definitions.
